# Core Product Thesis

FieldPlatform helps people, communities, creators, stewards, events, venues, practices, and generated patterns see how they are connected, where participation is held, and what movement is available next.

The product center is self-resourcing through field awareness. That means the platform helps people and communities resource themselves from the living field around them by making needs, impulses, possible supports, relations, and next steps visible enough to move.

FieldPlatform should be strongest at thresholds where people lose access to what is already around them:

- arrival
- transition
- stuckness
- unclear entry
- not knowing who to ask
- unresolved need
- invitation
- handoff
- afterglow
- memory
- digestion after something happened
- not knowing whether now is the right season

The app should help a person understand:

- what they are already involved in
- what they are following lightly
- where they have access, membership, trust, or commitment
- where a relationship is dormant or intentionally quiet
- what events, venues, communities, people, or generated patterns are nearby through overlap
- why a recommendation or connection appears
- what action is available now
- what still needs review, consent, boundary, capacity, or clarification

Everyone is a participant first. A person may also create an event, create a community, steward a group, facilitate a gathering, host, volunteer, or bridge communities. Those are contextual roles and capabilities, not separate base products.

## Product Center

The deeper center is connection, context, stewardship, and movement. FieldPlatform makes useful relations visible without turning community life into administration, surveillance, performance, or a content feed.

The practical product areas are:

- participant orientation across events, communities, relationships, and generated patterns
- connection context between events, communities, venues, people in creator/facilitator roles, and fields
- ways in, meaning next steps grouped by the object they act on
- steward review for suggested connections that need community care
- contextual disclosure after real interaction
- self-resourcing prompts for arriving, asking, offering, care, support, and digestion

Self-resourcing and field inquiry are core design direction, but many of their data concepts are still future surface pressure rather than stable collections.

## What The Product Is Not

FieldPlatform is not primarily an event marketplace, resource catalogue, CRM, productivity dashboard, social feed, organizer funnel, or a complete digital map of community life.

Its job is not to know everything. Its job is to help when the living field becomes hard to read.
