# Conviviality And Pilot Hypotheses

This page is parked planning reference for pilots, governance, resourcing, and validation. It is not an implementation contract unless a specific hypothesis is adopted into a product-definition page.

Conviviality itself is an active principle in [Living field principles](../Principles/Living%20field%20principles.md). This page preserves the less-settled planning hypotheses around how that principle might be tested, supported, governed, or resourced.

The former commercial business-case framing is retired. Commercial viability is not a design objective at this stage.

## Working Conviviality Hypothesis

FieldPlatform is valuable if it helps [people](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md) and [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) orient, meet, self-resource, and care for shared life from within a [living field](../Principles/Living%20field%20principles.md) of relations without requiring that field to be exhaustively mapped, constantly updated, or commercially exploited.

The job is to make thresholds easier to cross: where [people](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md) do not know where they can arrive, who to ask, what is beginner-friendly, which adjacent fields exist, what support is live, what care is needed, or what first step is appropriate.

## Primary Value

The platform should help:

- participants understand connection, access, trust, entry paths, and live support
- [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) make context, norms, boundaries, and entry guidance legible without becoming brands or catalogues
- creators/facilitators find fit without turning [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) into marketing segments
- [venues](../Data%20layer/Venue%20entity.md) and shared spaces surface care, asks, offers, rhythms, and next steps without chore scoring or social surveillance
- stewards notice suggested relations, [holds](../Ontology/Hold%20unclear%20point.md), and [pathways](../Ontology/Pathway%20ways%20in.md) without CRM behavior

## Commercialization Posture

If the platform later becomes financially self-sustaining, treat that as contingent on serving life well. Revenue, grants, sponsorship, paid services, or institutional support must remain subordinate to purpose, boundaries, and conviviality.

Do not let payment logic decide what the platform notices, who it serves first, what data it asks for, which [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) become visible, or whether participants are pressured to return.

## Pilot Learning Question

Does better relational orientation make participation more life-giving, more appropriate, and more [self-resourcing](../Modules/Self-resourcing%20Entry%20module.md) while keeping the tool quiet enough that direct life stays primary?

## Risks To Track

- generic [event](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md) listing
- organizer funnel
- CRM or surveillance
- marketing/ad-targeting [event](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md) creation
- suggestions as tagging, claiming, ownership, or social pressure
- generated patterns mistaken for [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md)
- premature monetization
- behavioral-data or retention logic
- digital double maintenance burden
- claims to represent the [living field](../Principles/Living%20field%20principles.md) more fully than possible
- living-space pilots turning proximity, meals, walks, and conversation into unnecessary app interactions
