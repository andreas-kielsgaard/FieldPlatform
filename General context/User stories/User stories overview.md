# User Stories Overview

A user story is an intention over time. It may move through several views and modules. It describes what capacity the platform enables.

Story groups:

- Orientation: understand what is already live and why events or communities appear.
- Self-resourcing: arrive with a need, impulse, offer, stuckness, care task, or unclear direction and find possible support without turning everything into project management.
- Belonging: understand relationships to communities at different depths.
- Community and field exploration: find appropriate ways into a community or generated field.
- Suggestion: propose that an event, venue, community, person, or pattern belongs in a context without claiming authority.
- Stewardship: review suggested relations, evidence, consequence, and visibility.
- Creation and management: create or manage events and communities with fit, access, and entry guidance in mind.
- Explanation: know why something appears, what relation exists, what is unclear, and what movement is available.
- Contextual disclosure: decide what becomes visible after real participation or before required logistical sharing.

If a proposed screen cannot trace to a user intention, it may be premature, too abstract, or better suited for parked exploration.
