# FieldPlatform For Communities

For communities, FieldPlatform makes clear that which is already known, but not yet shared.
A community is not only a name, page, or member list.
It has rhythm, trust, history, boundaries, practices, openings, and care.
FieldPlatform helps communities share enough of this for people to participate well, without turning community life into administration.

## Sharing What The Community Knows

You know which events are relevant to your communities.
You know which rules are important, how to start participating, the do's and don'ts, what is alive in your city, and where the pulse is.

You know who to trust and rely on, and who to go to if you want to be inspired.
You hold the experience of relational experiments gone right, and those that have gone wrong.
FieldPlatform gives communities a place to share this knowledge when sharing it serves participation and care.

## Making Entry Easier To Read

Many communities are hard to enter from the outside.

Someone may not know:

- whether newcomers are welcome
- where to begin
- which event is beginner-friendly
- who to ask
- what the norms are
- what preparation is needed
- what should not be assumed
- what kind of participation is appropriate now

FieldPlatform helps communities make this visible.
It should make entry clearer without flattening the community into a public listing.

## Stewarding Relation

Some connections need human review.
A suggested relation between an event and a community may be useful, but it may also carry context.
A person may be welcome in one setting and not another.
A venue may be right for one kind of gathering and wrong for another.

Stewards should be able to review relations before they become visible or actionable.
FieldPlatform should make stewarding clear:

- what is being suggested
- why it appears
- who can review it
- what approving it changes
- what remains private or uncertain
- how to say no without drama

This protects the community from becoming a public catalogue or CRM.

## Supporting Living Boundaries

Community belonging is rarely a simple yes or no.
Someone can be new, known, trusted, resting, returning, adjacent, responsible, invited, or not ready yet.
FieldPlatform should support these living edges without turning them into rigid labels.

It should help communities show enough for care and orientation while protecting what should stay contextual.

## What FieldPlatform Gives Communities

FieldPlatform gives communities:

- a place to show what is alive
- clearer ways for people to start participating
- ways to share do's, don'ts, rules, and rhythms
- steward review for suggested connections
- more control over boundaries and visibility
- a way to be found at the right threshold

FieldPlatform is successful when a community becomes easier to approach without becoming easier to consume.
