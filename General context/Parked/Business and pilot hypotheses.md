# Business And Pilot Hypotheses

This page is parked planning reference. It is not product-design authority and not implementation direction unless a specific hypothesis is adopted into a product-definition page.

The former commercial business-case posture is retired. Commercial viability is not a design objective at this stage.

## Working Conviviality Hypothesis

FieldPlatform is valuable if it helps people and communities orient, meet, self-resource, and care for shared life from within a living field of relations without requiring that field to be exhaustively mapped, constantly updated, or commercially exploited.

The job is to make thresholds easier to cross: where people do not know where they can arrive, who to ask, what is beginner-friendly, which adjacent fields exist, what support is live, what care is needed, or what first step is appropriate.

## Primary Value

The platform should help:

- participants understand connection, access, trust, entry paths, and live support
- communities make context, norms, boundaries, and entry guidance legible without becoming brands or catalogues
- creators/facilitators find fit without turning communities into marketing segments
- venues and shared spaces surface care, asks, offers, rhythms, and next steps without chore scoring or social surveillance
- stewards notice suggested relations, holds, and pathways without CRM behavior

## Commercialization Posture

If the platform later becomes financially self-sustaining, treat that as contingent on serving life well. Revenue, grants, sponsorship, paid services, or institutional support must remain subordinate to purpose, boundaries, and conviviality.

Do not let payment logic decide what the platform notices, who it serves first, what data it asks for, which communities become visible, or whether participants are pressured to return.

## Pilot Learning Question

Does better relational orientation make participation more life-giving, more appropriate, and more self-resourcing while keeping the tool quiet enough that direct life stays primary?

## Risks To Track

- generic event listing
- organizer funnel
- CRM or surveillance
- marketing/ad-targeting event creation
- suggestions as tagging, claiming, ownership, or social pressure
- generated patterns mistaken for communities
- premature monetization
- behavioral-data or retention logic
- digital double maintenance burden
- claims to represent the living field more fully than possible
- living-space pilots turning proximity, meals, walks, and conversation into unnecessary app interactions
