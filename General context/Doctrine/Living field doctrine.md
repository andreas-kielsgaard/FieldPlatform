# Living Field Doctrine

Do not digitize the living field. Digitize the thresholds where the living field becomes illegible.

The platform should help people return to contact with life, not make life serve the app. A digital representation is a partial trace of what is alive, not the field itself.

The living field is local, partial, relational, seasonal, and emergent. It includes communities, events, venues, practices, people, support, care, boundaries, trust, memory, rhythms, needs, offers, and adjacency that no platform can fully know.

FieldPlatform should therefore prefer situated exchanges over total models:

- a person names what they are arriving with today
- a steward reviews a specific suggested connection
- a community explains entry guidance
- a participant sees a first step
- a facilitator requests necessary logistical information for a specific event
- a group carries forward one useful nugget and lets the rest disappear

## Aliveness And Rhythm

The product should support contact rather than output alone. It should protect activation without coercion, engagement without dissociation, purpose without performance pressure, rest as participation, care for the space as participation, and body, need, relation, and emotion as part of the field.

Seasonal language is a design lens, not mandatory UI:

- spring: arrival, activation, orientation, state clarity, self-resourcing
- summer: creation, focus, doing the thing
- fall: digestion, selection, carry-forward, memory
- winter: rest, landing, soft togetherness, letting traces dissolve

The app is likely most useful around spring and fall thresholds. It should be quieter when proximity, conversation, meals, walks, room presence, rest, or doing the work already serves the movement better.

## Feasible Information Exchange

Do not design as if the platform can know every resource, capacity, contact, state, need, boundary, agreement, or readiness signal.

Useful information is information that can realistically and convivially enter the system:

- lightweight declarations people already want to make visible
- scoped asks and offers with immediate value
- signals that expire quickly
- relations reviewed where authority and consequence are clear
- selected nuggets from digestion
- support patterns intentionally carried forward

Every input needs an input bargain: what effort is asked, what value returns, how soon it returns, and why the app is the right medium.

## Convivial Tool Posture

The platform should enlarge people's ability to act, ask, join, care, rest, and understand. It should not become expert-controlled infrastructure that communities must obey to know themselves.

Anti-convivial drift appears when:

- the app asks people to maintain a digital double
- more data capture becomes the default answer
- stewards become CRM operators
- recommendations become manipulation
- care becomes scoring
- community life becomes a public catalogue
- proximity is replaced by unnecessary app mediation
