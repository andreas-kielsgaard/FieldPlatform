# What FieldPlatform Is

On the surface, FieldPlatform is a community and event network.
We connect participants with the practices and communities that they care about.
We empower those with creative vision and a desire to give something to the world with the tools, environments, and people that are ready to receive their offerings.
We make the field visible.

## Knowing The Field

FieldPlatform is designed around making the connections we have already established stronger, and making the connections right next to us visible.
Communities, creators, people, events, and locations exist all around us.
Some of them we already know.
Many of them are invisible to us until chance makes them known.

Maybe you have been enjoying a new hobby, and do not know that there are six other groups practicing the same thing in your city.
Maybe you enjoyed the friends you made at a workshop last week, and do not know that they all gather at a dance workshop every Friday.
Maybe you are travelling and want to find people that share the same practices as you in a new place.

FieldPlatform aims to make visible the network that exists between people.
It reveals the experiences, spaces, and relations that are already around us.
We believe the world is brimming with wonderful opportunities.
We believe that the nature of humans who see each other is to invite each other into aliveness.

Sometimes all you need is to see the hands that are reaching for you.
FieldPlatform is here to make that next step into life feel less like a leap of faith, and more like embracing a lover.

## Living In The Field

FieldPlatform is reciprocal by nature.
It works because people participate, and people participate because the platform gives something back.
In recognizing this, we intentionally avoid a lot of the design philosophy that has become common practice for similar products.

The ambition with FieldPlatform is not to design the most popular, highest market cap, strongest user retention product.
FieldPlatform wants you to participate with real life.
Whenever we choose whether or how to design a feature, we ask whether the choice will result in more or fewer people finding their way into a more alive way of being in the world.
Will the platform help unfold the possibilities afforded by life, or will it create another digital facade to keep up with?

We believe that healthy connections are based on invitation.
We design features to empower your field to invite you, and for you to invite your field.
We rely on you to share your knowledge of the field, so that we can share that knowledge with the field.

You know which events are relevant to your communities.
You know which rules are important, how to start participating, the do's and don'ts, what is alive in your city, and where the pulse is.
You know who to trust and rely on, and who to go to if you want to be inspired.
You hold the experience of relational experiments gone right, and those that have gone wrong.

You have been practicing how to be part of the world your whole life.
Through that, you have learned tools for connecting, resourcing, negotiating, and navigating life.
FieldPlatform gives you a place to share all of this, and to receive the wisdom that the field around you holds.

## Finding The Field

Not knowing exactly what you are looking for, or sometimes even that you are looking, and then finding it anyway, is one of the most exciting experiences in life.
When you share with the field the practices, communities, places, people, and curiosities that you like, the field answers by giving you more.
In new as well as familiar shapes.

FieldPlatform invites you to share how you participate in the field, and uses this information to make the field around you visible.
The practices, communities, facilitators, lineages, and people that you like help guide our understanding of what is alive, when, and where.
We want to use this information to guide you and those around you.

FieldOrganization wants to be a growing entity that does not feed off capital gains, but rather off the energy of people who care about the purposes we serve.
One of the ways this is manifest in FieldPlatform is how we treat your data.

We believe that the information you share with us is of most value if you are in control of how it is used.
Maybe you want it to be used for facilitators, communities, or people to find you.
Maybe you want us to use it to figure out what events to recommend to you.
Heck, maybe you even want us to sell it to advertisers.
Either way, the choice is yours.

Our mission is to empower the field, not the capitalist machine. We believe that starts with listening.

## Making Connections In The Field

Stepping into something new can be a challenge, and it needs to be treated with care.
That is why we treat new meetings as more than the switch of a button.
When connecting is an everything-or-nothing question, we either choose to isolate, or adjust our presence to the lowest common denominator.
We believe that this is one reason for the shallow nature of most social networks today.

Meeting new people in real life is an incredible process.
From the moment you enter a shared space, you start sensing the other.
Maybe you only see their body and share a few words.
Maybe you have an instant connection, and there is no doubt that you want to share your lives together.

By treating connection as gradual, FieldPlatform aims to make meeting new people and communities more transparent, effortless, and honest.
We want you to share exactly how you are present in the field you are in.
Not too much and not too little.

Perhaps you would like the people who attend the same events or communities as you to know your face, but not your name.
Perhaps you want to remain anonymous and keep connections offline.
Perhaps you want everyone you meet to know what practices you are excited about, and how to reach out to you.
Connection should come at the speed and the time that you are comfortable with, and FieldPlatform aims to make this easy.

## Three Ways Into The Platform

Everyone begins as a participant, but people meet the platform from different positions:

- [FieldPlatform for participants](FieldPlatform%20for%20participants.md): finding practices, communities, events, people, and next steps on your own terms.
- [FieldPlatform for creators](FieldPlatform%20for%20creators.md): making an offering visible to the people, communities, and places ready to receive it.
- [FieldPlatform for communities](FieldPlatform%20for%20communities.md): sharing entry knowledge, caring for boundaries, and stewarding relation.

These are not separate products. They are different ways of participating in the same field.

## What The Platform Includes

FieldPlatform is a community and event platform.
It exists to make clear that which is already known, but not yet shared.
The platform includes:

- a personal field view, where someone sees what they are part of and what can happen next
- community, [event](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md), and [venue](../Data%20layer/Venue%20entity.md) pages, where shared context is gathered in one place
- [ways in](../Ontology/Pathway%20ways%20in.md), which are clear next steps someone can take
- relationship context, which explains why people, places, communities, and events are connected
- review and consent points, where visibility or action waits for human judgment
- [self-resourcing](../Modules/Self-resourcing%20Entry%20module.md) prompts, where someone can name what they need, offer, notice, or want to carry forward

These parts make it easier to find and connect across the living landscape around you, on your own premises.
The aim is to make a better tool for communities, creators, stewards, facilitators, and people to share in the passion of being alive.
It is a tool that serves the currencies of authenticity and connection rather than those of finance and pretense.

You are invited to participate in our field.
We look forward to meeting you.
