# User Stories Overview

A [user](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md) story is an intention over time. It may move through several [views](../Frontend/Views%20overview.md) and [modules](../Frontend/Modules%20overview.md). It describes what capacity the platform enables.

Current story pages:

- [Orient to what is live](Orient%20to%20what%20is%20live.md): understand what is already live and why [events](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md) or [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) appear.
- [Self-resourcing and field inquiry](Self-resourcing%20and%20field%20inquiry.md): arrive with a need, impulse, offer, stuckness, care task, or unclear direction and find possible support without turning everything into project management.
- [Suggest Connection flow](Suggest%20Connection%20flow.md): propose that an [event](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md), [venue](../Data%20layer/Venue%20entity.md), [community](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md), [person](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md), or pattern belongs in a context without claiming authority.
- [Review Suggested Relation flow](Review%20Suggested%20Relation%20flow.md): review suggested relations, evidence, consequence, and visibility.

Story areas not yet split into dedicated pages:

- Belonging: understand relationships to [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) at different depths.
- [Community](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) and field exploration: find appropriate ways into a [community](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) or [generated field](../Data%20layer/GeneratedField.md).
- Creation and management: create or manage [events](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md) and [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) with fit, access, and entry guidance in mind.
- Explanation: know why something appears, what relation exists, what is unclear, and what movement is available.
- [Contextual disclosure](../Access%20layer/Contextual%20visibility%20and%20disclosure.md): decide what becomes visible after real participation or before required logistical sharing.

If a proposed screen cannot trace to a [user](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md) intention, it may be premature, too abstract, or better suited for parked exploration.
