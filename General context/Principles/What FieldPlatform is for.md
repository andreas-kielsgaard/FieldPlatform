# What FieldPlatform Is For

FieldPlatform helps [people](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md) notice what they are already connected to, what is available around them, and what next step is possible.

Sometimes the surrounding social field becomes hard to read.

A [person](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md) may not know where to arrive, who to ask, what is beginner-friendly, what support is live, what relationship exists, or what can happen next.

In this wiki, [self-resourcing](../Modules/Self-resourcing%20Entry%20module.md) means finding support, direction, and next steps from the life already around you. Field awareness means seeing enough of that surrounding context to act with care.

## Where It Should Be Strong

FieldPlatform should be strongest at thresholds where [people](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md) lose touch with what is already around them:

- arrival
- transition
- stuckness
- unclear entry
- not knowing who to ask
- unresolved need
- invitation
- handoff
- afterglow
- memory
- digestion after something happened
- not knowing whether now is the right season

## What A Person Should Be Able To See

The app should help a [person](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md) understand:

- what they are already involved in
- what they are following lightly
- where they have access, membership, trust, or commitment
- where a relationship is dormant or intentionally quiet
- what [events](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md), [venues](../Data%20layer/Venue%20entity.md), [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md), [people](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md), or generated patterns are nearby through overlap
- why a recommendation or connection appears
- what action is available now
- what still needs review, consent, boundary, capacity, or clarification

Everyone is a participant first.

A [person](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md) may also create an [event](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md), create a [community](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md), steward a [group](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md), facilitate a gathering, host, volunteer, or bridge [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md). Those are contextual roles and capabilities, not separate base products.

## Product Center

The deeper center is connection, context, stewardship, and movement.

FieldPlatform makes useful relations visible without turning [community](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) life into administration, surveillance, performance, or a content feed.

The practical product areas are:

- participant orientation across [events](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md), [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md), relationships, and generated patterns
- context for connections between [events](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md), [communities](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md), [venues](../Data%20layer/Venue%20entity.md), [people](../Data%20layer/Person%20entity.md) in creator or facilitator roles, and fields
- [ways in](../Ontology/Pathway%20ways%20in.md), meaning next steps grouped by the object they act on
- steward review for suggested connections that need [community](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) care
- [contextual disclosure](../Access%20layer/Contextual%20visibility%20and%20disclosure.md) after real interaction
- [self-resourcing](../Modules/Self-resourcing%20Entry%20module.md) prompts for arriving, asking, [offering](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md), care, support, and digestion

[Self-resourcing and field inquiry](../User%20stories/Self-resourcing%20and%20field%20inquiry.md) are core design direction. Many of their data [concepts](../Ontology/Product%20ontology.md) are still signals for future design, not stable data collections.

## What The Product Is Not

FieldPlatform is not primarily an [event](../Data%20layer/Event%20offering%20entity.md) marketplace, resource catalogue, CRM, productivity dashboard, social feed, organizer funnel, or a complete digital map of [community](../Data%20layer/Community%20entity.md) life.

Its job is not to know everything. Its job is to help when the [living field](Living%20field%20principles.md) becomes hard to read.
