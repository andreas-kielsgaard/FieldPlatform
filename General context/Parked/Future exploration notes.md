# Future Exploration Notes

This page preserves promising ideas that are not current implementation direction until explicitly adopted into product-definition pages.

## Aliveness Commons / Day-Retreat Space

The platform has a physical analogue in a shared morning threshold space, village square, day-retreat commons, or civic infrastructure for aliveness. This is not a coworking office with wellness attached. The useful principles are rhythm over ownership, rest and care as participation, contact over output alone, and rooms/tools as affordances rather than the organizing principle.

## Living House / Residency Slice

A bounded high-trust setting could test arrival prompts, asks, offers, care, digestion, field boards, and support patterns. It should not replace meals, walks, direct asking, or proximity; require complete resource catalogues; or turn a residency into project management.

Possible field board sections:

- people are looking for
- people can offer
- practices forming
- questions alive
- care needs
- quiet/focus energy
- soft/social energy
- things to carry into tomorrow

## Seasonal Rhythm

Spring for arrival and activation, summer for doing, fall for digestion and carry-forward, winter for rest. This is a design lens, not mandatory UI. Software may be most useful at spring/fall transitions and quieter during summer/winter.

## Environmental Prompts

Prompts can restore possibility without claiming to know the answer:

- What are you arriving with?
- What might nourish this?
- What wants support?
- Would it help to make this visible?
- Carry this into tomorrow, or let it disappear?

## Needs-First Recall

The better pattern is not a giant resource library. A person or group names a stuck point, the platform surfaces one or two possible tools, people, memories, or questions, and digestion decides whether anything should be remembered.

## Map And Rhythm Views

Maps and rhythm views may orient people through familiar territory, adjacent worlds, place, activity, and community rhythm. They should bridge from pattern into concrete doors: next events, communities, facilitators, venues, and ways in.

## Not Adopted

Not adopted as current product direction: full field visualization engine, exact map mode taxonomy, exact color blending strategy, detailed animation rules, home windows as committed UI, city/wider-field maps, and pilot KPIs as active product metrics.
