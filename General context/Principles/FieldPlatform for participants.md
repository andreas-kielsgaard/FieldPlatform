# FieldPlatform For Participants

For participants, FieldPlatform is a way to find the field around you.
It helps you see the practices, communities, people, events, and locations that are already near your life, but not yet visible to you.
The goal is not to keep you in the app.
The goal is to help you participate with real life.

## Knowing What Is Around You

Maybe you have been enjoying a new hobby, and do not know that there are six other groups practicing the same thing in your city.
Maybe you enjoyed the friends you made at a workshop last week, and do not know that they all gather at a dance workshop every Friday.
Maybe you are travelling and want to find people that share the same practices as you in a new place.

FieldPlatform aims to make these nearby relations visible.
It helps you see:

- communities you belong to, follow, or are near
- events and gatherings you can join
- practices you care about
- people you may know through a place, event, community, or role
- venues and places that matter to your field
- open invitations and possible [ways in](../Ontology/Pathway%20ways%20in.md)
- context for why something appears
- actions that are available now
- places where review, consent, boundary, or care is still needed

This should feel like finding the field, not like entering a feed.

## Sharing How You Participate

When you share with the field the practices, communities, places, people, and curiosities that you like, the field answers by giving you more.
This sharing does not need to be heavy.
It can be as simple as naming what you practice, what communities you are part of, what places matter to you, what you are curious about, or what you are looking for.

The practices, communities, facilitators, lineages, and people that you like help guide our understanding of what is alive, when, and where.
We want to use this information to guide you and those around you.

## Your Information

We believe that the information you share with us is of most value if you are in control of how it is used.
Maybe you want it to be used for facilitators, communities, or people to find you.
Maybe you want us to use it to figure out what events to recommend to you.
Heck, maybe you even want us to sell it to advertisers.

Either way, the choice is yours.
Our mission is to empower the field, not the capitalist machine.
We believe that starts with listening.

## Connecting At The Right Speed

Stepping into something new can be a challenge, and it needs to be treated with care.
That is why FieldPlatform treats new meetings as more than the switch of a button.

Perhaps you would like the people who attend the same events or communities as you to know your face, but not your name.
Perhaps you want to remain anonymous and keep connections offline.
Perhaps you want everyone you meet to know what practices you are excited about, and how to reach out to you.

Connection should come at the speed and the time that you are comfortable with, and FieldPlatform aims to make this easy.

## What FieldPlatform Gives Participants

FieldPlatform gives participants:

- clearer ways into communities and events
- better visibility into people, places, and practices nearby
- control over how they are present
- recommendations based on what they care about
- a place to ask, offer, rest, join, or stay private
- a way to receive the wisdom that the field around them holds

FieldPlatform is successful when the next step into life feels less like a leap of faith.
